KYA THEEK HUA
  1) "Sab players ne milke kitna khela" (Live Board)
     - Pehle: number beech-beech me RUK jata tha, phir ek dum jump karta tha,
       aur kabhi asli se 5-10 minute AAGE chala jata tha (galat).
     - Ab: har player ka time uski apni aakhri game report se gina jata hai.
       Jitne log khel rahe hain, total har second utna badhta hai.
       Report aate hi number server ke asli number pe aa jata hai, bina ruke.
     - Kabhi asli se zyada nahi dikhata, kabhi peeche nahi jata.
     - Card me ab likha aata hai: "X players abhi khel rahe hain, total har second +X sec".
       Koi nahi khel raha to: "Abhi koi nahi khel raha, isliye total ruka hai."
       (Koi nahi khel raha to total badhna hi nahi chahiye. Yahi asli data hai.)
     - Refresh karo ya dusre page pe jao aur wapas aao: wahin se chalta hai.
  2) Analytics > Top players
     - Jo player online hai, uske Hours har second badhte hain (seconds tak dikhte hain).
     - "Today" tab me bhi ab hours live badhte hain (pehle nahi badhte the).
     - Ads aur Races game ki report aate hi badhte hain (aur flash hote hain).
     - Search box me type karte waqt ab typing nahi kat-ti, aur list ka scroll
       apni jagah rehta hai (pehle har second table dobara banti thi).
  3) Analytics me 2 naye tiles: "Hours today (sab players)" aur
     "Hours all time (sab players)", dono har second live.
